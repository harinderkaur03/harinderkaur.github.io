Grameen Credit Score: a credit identity for rural borrowers

Problem

Hundreds of millions of rural and semi-urban borrowers in India repay loans reliably through Self-Help Groups and microfinance institutions, yet have no bureau score. Without one, they can't get formal loans.

What I did

  • Owned the Phase 1 backlog across data, AI/ML, engineering and lender-partner workstreams
  • Defined model acceptance criteria: score distributions, drift thresholds, accuracy benchmarks, and calibration to bureau-comparable ranges
  • Set up a flag-and-recheck loop with the ML team to validate scores against real repayment outcomes
  • Led go-live readiness through to the first lender queries in production
